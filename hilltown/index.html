      How the prices compare

      The Dundee averages include older and smaller flats that need work. The Hilltown flats are newly converted two-bedroom homes of 67–88 m², each with private outside space.

      Why the Hilltown

      • Rental demand59% of Dundee 2 bed homes let within a month, and 2 bed rents rose 8.7% in a year (Citylets).
      • Close to the centreAt the foot of the Hilltown, a short walk from the Wellgate Centre and the city centre.
      • Outside spaceEvery flat has a balcony or terrace, rare in Dundee's tenement flats.
      • Entry priceFrom £125,000, below the £145,000 LBTT threshold for a main home.

          Location

          112–114 Hilltown, DD3 7BG

          The building sits on Hilltown, one of Dundee's historic streets, which climbs north from the Wellgate towards the Law. Between a block of flats and a barber's, it looks south-west over the street; the balconies and terrace face north-east over the rear yard.
